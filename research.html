Research projects

Vision language learning for soybean seed quality

Developing and evaluating methods for recognizing purple seed stain symptoms with limited expert labeled data. My work includes dataset construction with SAM2 segmentation, label verification, zero shot CLIP, frozen feature classifiers, adapters, and LoRA fine tuning.

Efficient crop monitoring with edge AI

Studying soybean wilting severity classification from UAV imagery using super resolution and lightweight neural networks. Evaluation includes ablation studies, TensorFlow Lite quantization, and comparisons of accuracy, model size, and inference latency.

Environmental memory agents for outbreak prediction

Integrating USGS, NOAA, and NSRDB environmental records with harvest area and outbreak data. The framework uses lagged environmental features, shared historical memory, risk reasoning, and evidence verification for oyster associated norovirus outbreak prediction.
